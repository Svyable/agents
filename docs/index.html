THE PRODUCER / 2012–2013 ROOTS

Before the agents,
there was the remix.

Svyable is my DJ and electronic music producer identity. As part of the former duo PRFFTT & Svyable, I helped reimagine Ellie Goulding’s “Hanging On” and Taylor Swift’s “I Knew You Were Trouble.”

Those tracks belong to the early online remix era: a melody discovered, pulled apart, and rebuilt for a different kind of energy. That instinct still runs through the work.
